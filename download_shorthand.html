Shorthand Download
SUBMITTING YOUR ASSESSMENT

Package your Shorthand story for submission.

One bookmark saves a complete copy of your story, with its images and layout, as a single ZIP file you can submit. It runs in your own browser tab; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

An independent tool for submitting your work. It is not made by, or affiliated with, Shorthand.

3

Package your story

  1. a
    Open your Shorthand story (your published link or a Shorthand preview link) and scroll through all of it once so everything has loaded.
    ↕
  2. b
    Click the ↳ Download story bookmark. A panel appears on the page.
    ↳
  3. c
    Enter your name and student ID, then press Download my story. A ZIP file named using your details downloads. The panel’s Details list anything that could not be saved.
    ⇩
4

Check it, then submit

  1. a
    Extract the ZIP. Windows: right-click it and choose Extract All. Mac: double-click it. Inside the extracted files, open the story folder, then index.html, and check it matches your live story.
    ZIP
  2. b
    Submit the original ZIP file to Moodle, not the extracted folder.
    ✓

Good to know

  • Embedded videos, social posts and charts in your copy load live from the internet; their content is not saved in the ZIP.
  • Only Shorthand stories are supported. Pages on other sites, or with strict security settings, may block the bookmark.
  • Privacy: the bookmark reads the story page that is already open and downloads its own files and images directly from the published site. Nothing is sent to any other server, and there are no analytics.
