Two bookmarks to try

Shorthand Capture records a Shorthand story and packages it for assessment. Everything runs in your own browser tab; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

You need: desktop Chrome or Microsoft Edge (a recent version), and the bookmarks bar showing: Ctrl+Shift+B, or ⌘+Shift+B on a Mac. Safari, Firefox, tablets and phones are not supported for recording.

1. Download story TEST ONLY

Takes a copy of the story including images and script. Creates a zip file with a stand alone version. Useful for quickly checking how well different stories copy.

↳ Download Story Drag to your bookmarks bar
Can’t drag it?

Add a bookmark, name it Copy story (test) and paste this whole address as its URL.

How to test it

  1. Open a story and scroll through it once so everything has loaded.
  2. Click the Copy story (test) bookmark, then Make working copy. A ZIP downloads; the panel’s Details list anything that could not be saved.
  3. Extract the ZIP, open index.html and compare it with the live story, online and offline.

2. Capture story MAIN TOOL

Does the same as the aove but also allows you to make a screen recording of the story as a video, then downloads one ZIP containing the video, a working offline copy of the story and a capture record. This is what students would use.

↳ Capture story Drag to your bookmarks bar
Can’t drag it?

Add any bookmark, edit it, name it Capture story and paste this whole address (starting javascript:) as its URL.

How to test it

  1. Open a published Shorthand story (or a Shorthand preview link) and click the Capture story bookmark. A panel appears on the page.
  2. Press Record this page and choose Allow straight away (tick Share tab audio if the story has sound). The panel hides and a small Recording controls window opens on top.
  3. Scroll through the whole story at reading pace and play any media. Try Pause and Resume once.
  4. Press ■ Stop recording. If the controls window didn’t appear, click the bookmark again or use Chrome’s Stop sharing instead.
  5. Watch the recording in the panel, then press Capture & download package.
  6. Extract the whole ZIP (don’t open files from inside it). Play the video, open the capture record, and open index.html in the working-copy folder, with Wi-Fi on and then off.

Known limits

  • Embedded videos, social posts and charts in the working copy load live from the internet; their content is not preserved. The recording is the record of how they looked.
  • Chrome’s own “… is sharing this tab · Stop sharing” bar can’t be changed or hidden. It is not part of the video.
  • If you take more than a few seconds to choose Allow, the controls window may not open. Recording still works; stop with the bookmark or Chrome’s bar.
  • Long, high-quality recordings are held in memory until packaged. Very long recordings make packaging slower.
  • Only Shorthand stories are supported. Pages on other sites, or with strict security settings, may block the bookmarks.
  • If you previously installed an older version of either bookmark, delete it and drag the new one.

Privacy: the bookmarks read the story page that is already open and download its own files and images directly from the published site. Nothing is sent to any other server; no analytics. Only capture stories you are entitled to.
